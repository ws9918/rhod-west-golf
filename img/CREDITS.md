## Why not Rock Creek Cattle Company's own photos?

They are copyrighted by the club and their photographers. This repository is
public, so shipping them here would be republishing someone else's work without
a licence. These two were chosen for the same character instead: high country,
mountain backdrop, big sky.

The real answer for Rock Creek is your own photos from the round. Add them to a
rating (✎ on the rating, then Photos) and they become that course's images.
